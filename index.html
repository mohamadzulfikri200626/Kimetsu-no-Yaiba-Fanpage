KIMETSU NO YAIBA

Demon Slayer: the Blade of Demon Destruction.

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba is a Japanese manga series by Koyoharu Gotouge, adapted into an anime by Ufotable. It follows Tanjiro Kamado, who sets out to become a Demon Slayer after his family is slaughtered and his younger sister Nezuko is turned into a demon.

Explore Characters

About

Tanjiro Kamado is the eldest son of a family that sells charcoal. When a demon attack kills his mother and younger siblings and turns his sister Nezuko into a demon, he resolves to become a Demon Slayer in order to find a cure for her.

The Demon Slayer Corps is an organization that has existed since ancient times to protect humanity from demons. Its members act in secrecy, even from the Japanese government, and its strongest swordsmen are known as the Hashira, or Pillars. Behind it all stands Muzan Kibutsuji, the first demon, who attacked the Kamado family while seeking a demon that can withstand sunlight.

Anime Facts

  • The manga was written and illustrated by Koyoharu Gotouge.
  • It was serialized in Weekly Shōnen Jump from February 2016 to May 2020.
  • The chapters were collected in 23 volumes.
  • The anime by Ufotable began in 2019 and is directed by Haruo Sotozaki.
  • The film Mugen Train premiered in Japan in October 2020.

Characters

Tanjiro Kamado

The eldest son of a charcoal-selling family. After a demon attack kills his mother and younger siblings and turns Nezuko into a demon, he becomes a Demon Slayer to cure her.

  • Role: Demon Slayer
  • Technique: Water Breathing, Hinokami Kagura
  • Voice (JP): Natsuki Hanae

Nezuko Kamado

Tanjiro's younger sister, turned into a demon in the attack. She still sees humans as her kin and protects them from other demons.

  • Role: Tanjiro's sister
  • Trait: Sleeps instead of eating humans
  • Voice (JP): Akari Kitō

Zenitsu Agatsuma

A Demon Slayer who joined the Corps alongside Tanjiro. He seems fearful and anxious, yet fights effectively while unconscious.

  • Role: Demon Slayer
  • Technique: Thunder Breathing
  • Voice (JP): Hiro Shimono

Inosuke Hashibira

A Demon Slayer raised by boars, which left him feral and aggressive. He wears a boar's head as a mask and wields two serrated swords.

  • Role: Demon Slayer
  • Technique: Beast Breathing (self-taught)
  • Voice (JP): Yoshitsugu Matsuoka

Genya Shinazugawa

A Demon Slayer who cannot use breathing techniques. Instead, he temporarily gains a demon's powers by eating its flesh. He is the younger brother of the Wind Hashira.

  • Role: Demon Slayer
  • Weapon: Shotgun with Nichirin steel pellets
  • Voice (JP): Nobuhiko Okamoto

Kanao Tsuyuri

The adopted sister of Shinobu Kocho. Due to a traumatic childhood she once relied on a coin flip to make decisions, but learns to act on her own will.

  • Role: Demon Slayer (tsuguko)
  • Technique: Flower Breathing
  • Voice (JP): Reina Ueda

Murata

A member of the Demon Slayer Corps who survived the Natagumo Mountain operation and befriended Tanjiro. His determination helps him grow stronger in Hashira Training.

  • Role: Demon Slayer Corps member
  • Trait: Determined and hardworking
  • Voice (JP): Kōki Miyata

Giyu Tomioka

The Water Hashira and the first Demon Slayer Tanjiro meets. He spares Nezuko and sends the siblings to his mentor, Sakonji Urokodaki.

  • Role: Water Hashira
  • Technique: Water Breathing
  • Voice (JP): Takahiro Sakurai
